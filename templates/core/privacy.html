    Privacy notice

    What this CodeGuard deployment stores about you, why, who can see it and how to get it changed or removed. It describes what the software actually does; it is not a statement of compliance with any law.

    Who is responsible

    This deployment is run by its administrator, who decides who gets an account.{% if contact_email %} Contact: {{ contact_email }}.{% else %} No contact address has been published for this deployment yet; ask the person who invited you.{% endif %}

    What is stored

    • Account: username, email address, first and last name, a one-way hash of your password (never the password itself), role, how the account was created, whether and when your email address was confirmed, and an address you have asked to change to.
    • Profile and preferences: optional job title and team, and your display, accessibility and notification settings.
    • Your work: scenarios you write, simulations you run and their reports, and remediation items. Reports you share are visible to every signed-in user of this deployment.
    • Activity log: important actions (sign-ins, failed sign-ins, password and email changes, simulations, exports, sharing, role changes) with your username, the time, the object involved and your IP address. Passwords, tokens and session keys are never recorded. When you ask for a password reset or a confirmation email, a keyed fingerprint of the address is stored instead of the address.
    • Session: a session cookie and a security (CSRF) cookie, both needed to sign in. The session keeps when you signed in and a short browser description such as "Chrome on Windows", not the full browser string. Sessions end when you close the browser and after eight hours at most.
    • On your device: if you use the theme switch while signed out, your choice is kept in your browser's local storage. No advertising or analytics cookies are used.

    Why

    To let you sign in and recover your account, to run the product (your scenarios, simulations and reports), to protect accounts (sign-in limits, security notices, the activity log) and to let administrators investigate misuse.

    Who else receives data

    • Hosting: the server and its database are operated by the hosting provider of this deployment.
      • {% if email_provider %}
    • Email: confirmation links, invitations, password resets and security notices are delivered by {{ email_provider }}, which receives your address and the message.
      • {% endif %}

    Pages load everything, including fonts and icons, from this site; your browser is not sent to any third party. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

    Who can see it

    You can see your own account, work and activity. Administrators of this deployment can see the user list, every user's activity log and shared reports. Private scenarios and unshared reports are visible only to their owner.

    How long it is kept

    Account data and your work are kept while your account exists. Activity log entries are kept until an administrator removes old ones (the built-in retention command defaults to 365 days). Database backups are kept for a limited number of copies and then overwritten.

    Your choices

    • Download everything stored about you from Settings › Privacy and data.
    • Correct your name and details in Settings › Profile and your email in Settings › Account.
    • Close your account in Settings › Privacy and data. Sign-in stops at once; after {{ deletion_days }} days the account and everything you own (scenarios, simulations, reports including shared ones, remediation items, preferences) are deleted. Until then an administrator can reopen it.
    • Activity log entries are kept after deletion with the username they were recorded under, until the retention period removes them.
