    Terms of use

    The rules for using this CodeGuard deployment. Plain language; they do not replace any agreement your organization has with the operator.

    What CodeGuard is

    CodeGuard is a teaching and analysis tool. It models how a hypothetical compromise could spread through the stages of a software supply chain and estimates how much risk security controls remove. It never scans, attacks or connects to real systems.

    Results are model outputs

    Every score, path and recommendation comes from the documented rules on the methodology page, using illustrative values. Results are not a security assessment, a guarantee, or advice for any specific system, and they are provided as they are, without warranty. Decisions you make with them are yours.

    Your account

    • Keep your password to yourself and use one you do not use elsewhere.
    • Accounts are personal; do not share them.
    • Tell the administrator if you think someone else has used your account.

    Acceptable use

    • Do not enter real secrets, credentials, personal data about other people, or confidential details of real systems into scenarios, notes or names.
    • Do not try to access other users' private work, get around sign-in limits or roles, or disrupt the service.
    • Shared reports are visible to every user of this deployment; share only what everyone here may see.

    Access can end

    The administrator can change your role, suspend your account or remove it, for example when you leave the team or break these terms. The service may change or stop; keep copies of reports you need (CSV export).
